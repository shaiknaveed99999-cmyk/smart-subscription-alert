Privacy Policy

Smart Subscription Alert · Last updated 15 August 2026

Overview

Smart Subscription Alert is a local subscription and bill reminder app for Android. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with information on your device.

100% offline

The app is 100% offline. It does not create an account, does not require sign-in, and does not transmit data to any external servers. There is no cloud sync, analytics backend, or remote API.

Local storage only

Subscription names, amounts, due dates, categories, and optional payment links are stored on this device with AsyncStorage. Optional JSON backups that you export stay under your control (share sheet or a file you choose). Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

No personal data collection

The app does not collect personal data. It does not read the SMS inbox, does not access contacts, location, or your call log, and does not upload data from other apps. Local notifications are scheduled on-device so reminders can fire after a reboot.

Notification Access (Smart Passbook)

The app requests Notification Access solely to identify bank and subscription transaction alerts. That access is used only for the optional Smart Passbook feature, which you enable in Android Notification access settings.

The parsing happens 100% locally on the device. Matching alerts are converted into subscription records stored in AsyncStorage on this phone.

No notification data, SMS content, or financial information is ever transmitted to a server, shared with third parties, or stored externally.

No third-party sharing

The app does not share data with third parties. Optional UPI tip actions only open a payment app you already have installed, using a developer-configured payee ID. That handoff is initiated by you and is not a data-collection pipeline.

Permissions

  • Post notifications — upcoming bill reminders
  • Schedule exact alarms — timed local alerts
  • Receive boot completed — restore reminders after restart
  • Notification access (optional) — Smart Passbook local parsing of bank and subscription alerts

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the app listing on the Google Play Store.
